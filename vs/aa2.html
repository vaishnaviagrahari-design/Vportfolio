About me

"HI, I'm Vaishnavi Agrahari, a knitwear designer currently studying designing at NIFT Bengaluru. Fashion has been a part of my life for as long as I can recall. Even as a young girl, I was fascinated by the way clothes could change a person's mood and confidence. This lifelong love for creating and designing led me to pursue fashion formally. I love the process of taking a simple idea and turning it into something wearable, playing with different colors and textures to create pieces that feel special."
